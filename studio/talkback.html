Repeat-back lab

Record a short phrase, then hear it repeated. This experiment runs on your computer, separately from autonomous firmware. Try the optional automatic pilot after a successful manual test. This page repeats what it hears without filtering words.

Start at low volume. On one Ultra Skelly, the owner confirms manual playback and both automatic pilot phrases with jaw movement, followed by the pilot stopping. Longer sessions and recovery remain Untested.

Deep plays at 80% speed with a lower pitch; it adds no echo or extra repeats. The voice applies to manual and automatic playback. Deep voice is not yet tested on Skelly.

Automatic repeat-back pilot · new

Listen for nearby speech, capture a phrase, turn the microphone off, then repeat it. The pilot stops after two repeats or two minutes, with a 45-second pause between repeats. Stay quiet for the first second while it measures background noise. A pause of about 0.8 seconds ends a phrase; capture stops at eight seconds.

This uses sound level, so music, wind or other noise can trigger it. Keep this page visible. Start with computer speakers, then an attended Skelly test after checking the actual output. Stop & discard ends the pilot immediately.

Microphone and speaker setup

The laptop handles recording and detection; no ESP32 microphone is needed for this lab. Use your laptop’s built-in or USB microphone. A Bluetooth speaker’s microphone may require a separate hands-free mode; it is not automatically available through its music connection.

Choices below apply only to this repeat-back tab. Halloween conversation uses the microphone and output selected in Windows; these choices do not carry over.

Input: computer default. Choose microphone lists the inputs exposed by your browser without opening a recording. If names are hidden, record a phrase and allow access first, then choose microphone again. Selections apply to manual and automatic capture and last only in this tab.

Output: computer default. Choose speaker opens a browser picker or lists available outputs below. If only the default is listed, record a phrase and allow microphone access first, then choose speaker again. You can also choose the output in Windows Sound settings. Pair Bluetooth speakers there first.

For jaw testing, output must go to Skelly’s own (Live) speaker. An external speaker alone does not establish jaw synchronization. Check the actual sound location before raising volume.

Isolated Ultra Skelly live-audio test

6.5-foot Ultra Skelly v2 only. Let any movement finish, unplug the autonomous ESP32, including its USB cable, and close other prop-control apps. Leave Skelly in Bluetooth mode. Keep its power switch accessible; stop if any unexpected movement occurs.

Already connected? If Windows has Ultra Skelly v2(Live) Stereo connected and selected, and sound actually comes from Skelly, skip Enable and pairing. You can use repeat-back or Halloween conversation directly. A saved pairing alone does not confirm the speaker is connected.

Use Enable when Skelly’s Live speaker is unavailable. This sends one live-enable request, then releases BLE. Windows handles the separate audio connection. Nothing connects until you choose Enable; failures are not retried automatically.

  1. If the Live speaker is missing or unavailable, choose Enable live audio and select your prop by advertised name. Each discovery step can take up to 30 seconds. Keep this page visible until it says Live-enable write completed; switching away during setup cancels it. A completed write alone does not confirm sound or jaw response.
  2. Then connect the saved Live speaker in Windows Bluetooth settings. For first pairing, open Settings → Devices → Bluetooth & other devices → Add Bluetooth or other device → Bluetooth in Windows 10. Select Skelly’s (Live) speaker; the main settings list only shows devices already added.
  3. If asked for a PIN, try 1234, the default documented by the community integration. Use your configured PIN if changed; DecorPro’s Edit Name section shows it. After pairing, select the speaker as your computer’s audio output.
  4. Record and repeat one short phrase. Watch for sound from Skelly, jaw response, and any unintended body motion. For Halloween conversation, select the laptop microphone and Skelly Stereo output in Windows before opening that page; its Check local engine button checks only the local reply service.

After testing: Stop & discard, disconnect the computer from the (Live) speaker, and power-cycle Skelly before turning the autonomous ESP32 back on. Closing this page does not disable the prop’s live mode or confirm motors stopped.
