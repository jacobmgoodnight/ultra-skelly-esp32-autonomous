Help and returning to normal operation

Stay quiet for a second after choosing Talk, then speak and pause. Each capture lasts up to eight seconds. Compare your words with What Skelly heard.

Stop ends recording and playback in the browser; sound already queued over Bluetooth may finish. Chat memory stays on this laptop and expires after five idle minutes.

Family mode plays only reviewed replies. Open mode generates replies and can say things nobody reviewed; use it only while you are listening. In cloud mode, raw microphone audio stays local; Claude receives recognized speech and recent chat, and ElevenLabs receives Skelly’s reply text. Provider billing and retention terms apply.

Back to normal: choose New visitor, choose Stop and exit in the launcher, disconnect the Live speaker in Windows, power-cycle Skelly, then plug the ESP32 back in and watch one normal routine.
