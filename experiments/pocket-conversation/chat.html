Speaker setup & help

Select Ultra Skelly v2(Live) Stereo as the speaker and your chosen Windows microphone as input. Keep the ESP32 unplugged, including USB, and other control apps disconnected. Start at low volume and stay with the test.

If the Live speaker is missing, use the repeat-back lab’s Enable live audio setup. Wait for Live-enable write completed, connect Skelly in Windows Bluetooth settings, and select its Stereo output. For first pairing, follow the prop pairing instructions.

Stay quiet for one second after starting, then speak and pause. A capture lasts up to eight seconds. Automatic sensitivity adapts to room noise; competing voices may still confuse recognition. Compare your words with What Skelly heard above.

Stop ends browser recording and playback but cannot physically halt an animation already sent to Skelly; queued Bluetooth sound may continue. New visitor, changing the service or personality, or leaving this page clears the visit. Chat is held in memory and expires after five idle minutes.
